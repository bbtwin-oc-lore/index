Bingo comic lore:  
Oro/Owo heaven/hell are different than biblical heaven/hell which was destroyed a while ago.  
  
Oro's hell is corporate hell, the comic is about Damnation Corporation which was founded by a demon guy named Grampus Weisenheimer, grandpad of Vanguard Weisenheimer Jr.  
Main cast:  
Vanguard Weisenheimer Jr: Main character and CEO. He's like a Michael Scott character, incompetent egotistical insecure. He originally represented pride.  
Eira Brimrock: He used to work under Grampus, he's the COO and was Grampus' assistant. He's grumpy and really cares about the corporation, he is a suckup and takes his job seriously and runs things more than Weisenheimer.  
Malody Mergomeiser: also worked with Grampus, she wanted to be the next CEO but she didn't get it because of nepotism and hates Vanguard. She's a smarter, more competent version of Zim, but hides her hate more. She is primarily driven by wanting to be CEO. Represented Wrath.  
Minor Characters:  
Behema Baraphnam: big hairy orange demon lady, kinda a bully character, she usually uses her words instead of her strength. She's an accountant and decent at her job Gluttony  
Filthy Lucher: Green bird character thing. His real name is different because he stole someones identity and lied to get hired. He's very bad at his job. He's an amateur rapper and he's only kept around because Vanguard thinks he's funny Greed  
Bradapus Dwinnmor: Sloth. Physically a sloth. Straightman, boring, always tired, only complains, kinda a bitch not gonna lie.  
Animus Goatson: Little satyr. Loves his job. Really respects Vanguard and doesn't see how incompetent he is. Clumsy/ditzy but is dedicated to his job  
Vermin B Noser: Real lastname is "Brownnoser", intern.
