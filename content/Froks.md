Frox is one of the two main gods in Twins' lore, created by Bongo. Frox created the metaphysical world of Owo, and is able to create things from nothing.


### Lore
When Frox found himself in the white void of Owo he decided to create all things and concepts out of excitement. When Frox felt lonely in his world he created the first [[Screature]], [[Joey]], in his image, who he treated as a friend and advisor. He created more people at Joeys request, initially creating pure-bred Screatures thinking they would mix, but many didn't, creating separate races. (Canon note: pure-bred single-animal screatures were only mentioned once on March 30th as far as I know.) When Joey died Frox realized that he never created a place for people to go after they died. He visited Joey's house and discovered a [Bible](Christianity), which gave him the idea to create [[Heaven]] and [[Heaven and Hell]] in Owo.
