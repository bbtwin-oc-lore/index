
Oro and Owo both have their own versions of Heaven and Hell. The Christian versions of Heaven and Hell existed in the Ringworld but were destroyed a long time ago. This does not prevent the real world's Christianity from existing in the Ringworld however, as [[Joey]] was given a copy of the Bible by [[The Eye]].
### Hell in Oro
Oro's hell is not necessarily a place where people go to get punished so much as it is a corporate society inhabited by demons and other creatures. It is the setting of [[Damnation Corporation]].
